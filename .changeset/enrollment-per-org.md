A member of several organizations with protected environments gets their first passkey once an admin of each has approved, each on their own Access tab. It used to take one person who was an admin of all of them, so a member of two organizations could get stuck. The Access tab shows what's still missing, and so does the member's Passkeys page.

Anyone can now leave an organization under **Organization settings** in the sidebar, and auto-join doesn't add them back. The last admin can't leave.
